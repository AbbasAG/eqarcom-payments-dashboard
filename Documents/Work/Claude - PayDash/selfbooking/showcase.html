Platform Features

Cross-cutting capabilities that enhance every journey.

🔄

Real-Time Inventory Sync

Unit availability, pricing, and payment plans are synced from Salesforce via the EQARCOM integration. No stale listings.

💬

AI Chat Assistant

Floating chat button on every page. Tenants can ask about units, payment plans, community info, or get help navigating the experience.

🗺️

Community Search

Auto-completing search across all communities in the portfolio. Tenants find properties by neighbourhood, not just building name.

📋

Flexible Payment Plans

1, 4, 6, or 12 payments per year — displayed clearly on every unit. Monthly payment properties get a promotional highlight.

🇦🇪

UAE Pass Integration

Emirates ID verification via UAE Pass for a seamless, secure KYC process — or traditional document upload as a fallback.

🏠

Floor Plans & Virtual Tours

3D floor plans with pinch-to-zoom viewer and virtual tour links for units that have them. Full unit visualization before visiting.
